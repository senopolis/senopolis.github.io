Privacy Policy — Timeline Tactics

Last updated: 2026-09-21

Timeline Tactics has no accounts and collects no personal information. When you connect to play, the server gives your session a random, throwaway name and icon (like "Iron Drake") just so a match has something to display — it isn't linked to you and disappears the moment you disconnect. The server also keeps a short, in-memory list of recent match results so the lobby can show recent activity; nothing in it identifies you.

We don't use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking. The only connection the app makes is a direct, encrypted connection to our own game server, used solely to relay moves during a match.
